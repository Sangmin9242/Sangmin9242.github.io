# Site Life Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Extend the maltipoo's daily life into the office and building, and let visitors select each parked car and start a worker's return trip.

**Architecture:** Keep autonomous dog navigation, vehicle geometry and the vehicle trip state machine in separate modules. The main scene coordinates camera focus, UI and pause rules. Use the existing Three.js runtime and bilingual controls.

## Constraints

- Preserve research areas, CV records, sports games, weather and visitor counts.
- Office floor top is world y=.102, building ground slab top y=.08, parking top y=-.075 and terrain y=-.135.
- Dog may enter the open first floor; it must use clear walkways, avoid furniture/columns, and sleep inside the office. Toileting happens outdoors.
- Only one vehicle trip runs at a time. Clicking a car opens its card; boarding starts only after clicking its boarding button.
- A worker walks out of the building, approaches the driver's door, sits in the cabin, reverses out, drives away, returns, parks and walks back. Camera following can be stopped while the trip continues.
- All controls must remain usable on narrow screens and in Korean/English. Pausing the tab must not skip boarding or teleport vehicles. Reduced motion must retain the interaction.

## Deliverables and checks

- [x] Dog navigation/pose: inside-office home, broad obstacle-aware routes, sleeping, running, urination and crouched defecation. Keep pet/treat/call behavior. Test frame-rate consistency, floors and interruption states.
- [x] Car geometry: movable driver's door and visible cabin, spinning wheels and front steering, individual lamp materials. Keep existing white models/plates.
- [x] Pure vehicle trip: walk/open/board/close/reverse/depart/away/return/park/exit/walk-home phases, all five vehicles, re-entry guards, deterministic poses and safe routes.
- [x] Scene/UI: click car → bilingual card → board button; scene following and manual view; no control overlap; keep the dog lawn corridor behind the vehicle turning envelope.
- [x] Browser validation: office dog, sleep/toilet poses, all car selections, full departure/return, mobile resize, language, page navigation and existing games.
- [ ] Review, publish through the existing GitHub Pages repository, and verify the deployed site.

Validation: model bounds/door apertures, 250 dog pose samples, toilet prop fall/cleanup, three browser widths (1440/1024/390), full trip, direct car click, photo and language controls.
